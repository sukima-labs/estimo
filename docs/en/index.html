¿Es timo? closed

It checked a rental listing before you paid anything. It stopped on 15 November 2026: almost nobody ended up using it.

What it was

It took the listing and the chat with the «landlord» (text or screenshots) and returned a green, amber or red light, the reason for each warning sign and what to check. One model read the screenshots, another the text, and a third step tried to overturn the verdict before it was given. It never said anyone was a scammer: it said the pattern appears in known scams.

What we measured

  • Precision of red: in 74 runs over 37 invented cases, no legitimate ad came out red and 82.5 % of the scams were marked red. We wrote those cases ourselves: they say nothing about real ads.
  • Cost: about €0.0004 per report (the target was under €0.05).
  • Real use: two analyses in production in six weeks, one on Telegram and one on the web on opening day. Nobody answered the follow-up.

Why it closed

It is a tool for one moment, right before paying, and the channels we tried do not reach that moment: housing groups (we were muted in three of four), emails to people who help flat-hunters, a channel of our own, and Google searches, which brought more guide readers than users. We chose to close it rather than keep a service nobody uses.
