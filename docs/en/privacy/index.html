Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

This site

It is static: no forms, no cookies, no analytics, and it stores nothing about visitors. It is served by GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses under its own policy.

The service that closed

While it ran (a Telegram bot from 1 October 2026 and a website from 14 October), it processed the listing and the conversation each person sent, with an OpenAI AI model reached through OpenCode, and kept irreversible fingerprints (HMAC) of advertisers' phones, IBANs, emails and names to recognise repeated ads. It stopped on 15 November 2026.

The service's data will be deleted before 29 November 2026 (planned date). Everything goes: texts and reports, fingerprints, photo fingerprints, the listings copy used for price comparison, usage logs and the anonymous case sheets.
