Finding a room for the academic year

The start of every semester is peak season for room scams in Spain: high demand, pressure to decide fast, and many people searching from another city or country.

The most common scams

  • A Bizum (Spain's instant phone payment) or transfer to «hold» the room before you see it. More in asked for a deposit before viewing.
  • The «landlord abroad» who sends the keys by courier.
  • A very cheap room near the university, with photos copied from another listing.
  • A fake hall of residence or platform that «holds the money» until you arrive.
  • They ask for your passport or enrolment letter before showing you anything. More in asked for your ID or payslip.

If you are searching from abroad

  • Book a few nights of temporary accommodation and look for the room once you arrive: seeing it in person rules out almost every scam.
  • If you cannot, ask someone you trust to visit for you. A video call is not enough.
  • Ask your university: many have a housing list and an accommodation or international students office that can advise you. Even so, the same rules apply to any listing.
  • If a hall of residence asks you to pay, find its official website yourself (not through a link they send you) and check that the account is theirs.

Before you pay

  • Visit the room and the flat, and if you can, meet the people who live there.
  • Sign a contract that states the room, the rent, the deposit and who receives it.
  • Pay into an account in the name of whoever signs the contract, and keep the receipt.
