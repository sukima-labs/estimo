How it works

  1. Paste the listing (text or link) and your conversation with the advertiser, or upload screenshots. Spanish or English, as it is.
  2. You get a green, amber or red light, the reason for each sign, and what to check.
  3. If we suggest a question for the advertiser, add their answer and we look at it all together again.

Also on Telegram: @EsTimoBot (it answers in English if your Telegram is in English, or type /english).

What it does and does not do

  • It tells you whether the case looks like known scams: money before a viewing, a landlord abroad, keys sent by courier, a price far below the area…
  • It never claims that anyone is a scammer. A warning is a reason to check, not an accusation.
  • It sells nothing, shows no flats and contacts nobody on your behalf.
  • It is being tested: if you spot a mistake, telling us at info@patriciollorens.com helps a lot.

The golden rule

Do not pay anything, not even a booking fee or deposit, before visiting the property and signing a contract. In the Comunidad de Madrid the deposit («fianza») is lodged with the Agencia de Vivienda Social, and you can ask for the receipt.

Privacy, in short

  • Screenshots are read for the analysis and discarded.
  • The text of the case and the report are kept for 30 days and then deleted.
  • The advertiser's phone numbers, IBANs and names are kept only as an irreversible fingerprint, to recognise listings that repeat.
  • The analysis is done by an OpenAI AI model that does not train on this data.

All the details are in the privacy policy. Are you a landlord and think your details are in our registry by mistake? Write to us and we will stop using them.
